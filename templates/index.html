    📋 Scoring Framework & Standards Guide 
    [Classification Levels]
- low_complexity      : Extremely simple signature, minimal strokes or initial (0-30)
- medium_complexity   : Typical everyday signature with recognizable structure (31-65)
- high_complexity     : Large number of strokes, rich structural details (66-100)

[Signature Types]
- english_signature   : Primarily Latin characters / alphabetic cursive
- chinese_signature   : Primarily Chinese character structures
- mixed_or_unclear    : Combined characteristics or undetermined language
- unknown             : Insufficient information (typical for low complexity)
